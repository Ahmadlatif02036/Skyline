A little more about Skyline Pro.

Weather for the places you care about, presented with a little more clarity. Search anywhere, save your cities, and see what the day has in store.

Conditions and air quality are model estimates, rather than measurements from a station at your exact location. Local times follow the selected place's time zone. Forecasts update when you refresh and every 15 minutes while this page is open. The outdoor window is a suggestion derived from the forecast, rather than an official alert. Map tiles are supplied by OpenStreetMap.

Saved places, units, and your theme stay in this browser. Location permission is requested only when you choose “My location”.
